## 0. The 5 strongest numbers (use these first)
1. **83%** of payments at the till are digital [S11], yet **17M+** paper receipts came from just 600+ Dutch horeca venues in 2025 [S1].
2. **2 out of 3** Dutch companies say receipts are **regularly missing** from expense claims [S12] ⚠️ ICS-commissioned.
3. **7 years**: every receipt must stay complete, authentic and checkable [S2].
4. **€0 VAT back** on a client lunch: horeca food/drink VAT is not deductible [S15], and the cost itself is only partly deductible (80%, or above a €5,700 threshold) [S15].
5. **41 hours/month**: average manual expense processing at the 25%+ of Dutch companies still doing it by hand [S12] ⚠️.

## 3. Legal: a receipt is a tax document (all official Belastingdienst)
- Keep it for **7 years** (10 for real estate). [S2]
- Scanning is OK if the copy is complete and correct and the **authenticity features** are kept. [S2]
- Scanned **cash** receipts: keep the original unless agreed with the Belastingdienst. [S2b]
- A till receipt is a valid **simplified invoice up to €100** incl. VAT. [S16]
- Horeca food/drink VAT is **not deductible**. [S15]
- Business lunch (income tax): the first **€5,700 isn't deductible**, or deduct **80%**; corporate tax uses **73.5%**. [S15]

## 4. Processing
- **25%+** of companies process expense claims manually (mostly 1–50 employees), at **41 h/month**. [S12] ⚠️
- **28%** still have employees pay upfront and hand in paper receipts. [S12] ⚠️
- 41.4% of Dutch entrepreneurs say they spend half their week or more on admin. [S21] ⚠️ self-reported, n = 209
- *Old but telling:* in 2013, 25% didn't claim because of a lost receipt [S14] ⚠️ 2013

## 4b. Warranty side (secondary, consumer)
- For warranty, a bank statement can serve as proof of purchase if the receipt is lost. [S24]
- **3 out of 4** stores gave wrong or incomplete warranty information in a Consumentenbond mystery-shopping test. [S23] ⚠️ 10 companies tested
- Use only as a "next step" or Q&A angle: the receipt also matters years later for warranty on business purchases.

## 4c. Adjacent signals (verified, use sparingly)
- **90%** of consumers want warranty info on the receipt; **59%** with a defect didn't claim. [S34] ⚠️ panel
- **13%** of checked promo items were mispriced at the till (6 regional supermarkets). [S27] ⚠️
- **70.11M** return parcels in NL in 2025 (11.4%). [S28]
- **215,454** ISDE subsidy applications in 2025, each needing invoices, payment proof and photos. [S35]
- ❌ Don't say "62% missing receipts". The correct figure is **two-thirds**. [S12]

## 5. Why now
- NL: mandatory **B2B** e-invoicing from **1 Jul 2030**, which doesn't cover till receipts as far as the letter says. [S9]
- Germany: the cabinet approved digital till receipts from **2028**. [S18] France, Italy and Austria are already moving. [S22]
- The NL has **no** digital-receipt rule; retailer e-bons aren't tax-valid (Hornbach). [S19]

## 6. Competition (minesweeper)
- We mapped **15+ products** in 6 categories. [competitors.md]
- OCR scanning is a commodity: Moneybird includes it from **€3/month**. [C1]
- Receipt ↔ bank matching: Jortt launched it on **9 Oct 2026**. [C3]
- In the products we looked at, we **didn't find** any that check receipts against Dutch fiscal rules or capture the business context at the moment of payment.

## The story (draft)
> A Dutch freelancer takes a client to lunch. They pay with their phone, like 83% of payments today, but still get a paper receipt. By law, that slip is a tax document they must keep checkable for 7 years. The VAT on it can't be deducted, the cost only partly, and only if they can show who they ate with and why. Two out of three companies say receipts regularly go missing. Scanning apps exist (Moneybird does it for €3 a month), but none of the 15+ tools we checked tell you whether that receipt will hold up in an audit.
