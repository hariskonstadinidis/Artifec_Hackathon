### [S9] NL implementation of ViDA: Contourenbrief e-facturatie (Grant Thornton) ✅
- URL: https://www.grantthornton.nl/insights/tax/btw/nederland-zet-in-op-brede-uitrol-van-e-facturatie-en-digitale-btw-rapportage/
- Letter of 11 Sep 2026 from Staatssecretaris Eelco Eerenberg. Mandatory e-invoicing for domestic **and** intra-EU B2B from **1 July 2030**; digital reporting for domestic B2B from **1 July 2031**.

- ⚠️ The text covers **B2B only**. It does not mention B2C, till receipts or simplified invoices.

### [S12] ICS × PanelWizard: expense-claim management (Accountancy Vanmorgen, 9 Oct 2024) ✅⚠️
- URL: https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2024/10/09/nog-steeds-veel-bedrijven-die-declaraties-handmatig-verwerken/

## Pitch & MVP best-practice sources

### [P1] Reskilll: "Hackathon Demo and Presentation Tips: How to Pitch in 3 Minutes and Win" ✅
- URL: https://blogs.reskilll.com/hackathon-demo-presentation-tips-pitch-3-minutes-win-2026/
- Timing: 0:00–0:20 hook (problem plus one statistic), 0:20–0:40 solution, 0:40–2:30 live demo, 2:30–2:50 tech and impact, 2:50–3:00 close. Record a backup video. No code editor on screen. Don't demo half-built features. Don't run over time. Don't end with "so… any questions?"
- What judges ask themselves: does it work, would people use it, is the UX intuitive, is the tech interesting, can it scale?

### [P2] Magomed Kurbaitaev: "What judges actually score" (dev.to) ✅⚠️
- URL: https://dev.to/kurbaitaev/what-judges-actually-score-notes-from-a-year-of-hackathon-judging-3p4l
- Winners separated on (1) a working live demo, (2) saying what it is, for whom, in the **first sentence**, and (3) scope that fits the time. Judges ask: who exactly is it for, what did you build during the event, what breaks first? Admitting a known weakness helped scores. Polish and slide decks didn't move scores much.
- ⚠️ One judge's first-hand notes from three hackathons.

### [P3] Hackathon scoping guides ✅
- Builder's Guide ch. 9: https://intercom.help/builderbase/en/articles/14836119-builder-s-guide-ch-09-scoping-your-project
  - Teams finish about 25% of their plan, so "divide it by four". Build a "minimum viable proof": one core user, one problem, one flow. Fake or hardcode everything outside it. Cut the remaining list in half at the halfway point.
- Newly, "What to build in a hackathon" (Oct 2026): https://newly.app/guides/what-to-build-in-a-hackathon
  - "Anything needing accounts, a second device, a payment, or an API you have never called spends hours you do not have."

### [P4] Michael Seibel (Y Combinator), via SaaStr ✅
- URL: https://www.saastr.com/the-y-combinator-guide-to-perfectly-pitching-your-seed-stage-startup-with-michael-seibel
- The two-sentence test. "80% accurate and 100% clear." Lead with your strongest point. Plain slides.
