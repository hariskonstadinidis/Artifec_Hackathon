Artifec, an AI and automation partner company based in The Hague, is organising a Hackathon together with the programming school Codam in Amsterdam. The idea is simple: give students a real business problem to work on, and find new talent to join our team.

Dates: October 16th - October 19th

Agenda: The hackathon will run over four days, from Friday to Monday, at Codam.

Day 1: On Friday we kick off at 14.30 in the auditorium with an introduction to Artifec, the challenge and the rules, and teams start building straight away.

Day 2 & 3: Saturday and Sunday are build days, with our CTO around for questions.

Day 4: On Monday we will start the closing event at 15.00 in the auditorium every team presents their solution, and the winner is announced the same day.

The challenge: make the receipt problem disappear for businesses. Every client lunch, taxi ride or parking ticket leaves a small paper receipt that someone has to collect, scan, book and keep for years. Teams decide who they build for and which part of the problem they want to solve. They can use any tools they like, from code and AI to no-code platforms and existing APIs, as long as they start from scratch.

The hackathon is open to Codam students at Rank 05 of the Common Core or higher, and to TechMeUp students. Participants work in teams of two. Students can form their own team, and anyone who signs up alone will be paired with someone at the kickoff. Sign-up will go through Codam.

On the final day, each team presents to Artifec's CEO and CTO. A working demo is welcome, but not required. The winning team will be offered a paid internship at Artifec. More details, including the exact dates and how to sign up, will be shared soon.

BEST PART? There is a potential internship that will be given to the winner! So, looking for a job? Join this hackathon!
